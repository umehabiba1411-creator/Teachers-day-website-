🌷 Happy Teachers' Day, my dear Ma'am! 🌷

It has been quite a long time since I left school, but some teachers and some memories are so special that they never truly leave your heart, no matter how much time passes. ❤️

I joined school in 7th grade and spent my journey from 7th to 10th grade with all of you. That time has passed, and life has now entered a new phase, but whenever I remember my school days, you are always a part of those beautiful memories because you are special to me. ❤️

You used to call me “Angry Bird.” 😂 At that time, I probably used to make faces whenever you said that. 😂 But now, when I look back, these little moments have become some of my sweetest memories. 🥹❤️

And honestly, you were the one and only teacher in the whole school whom I was actually scared of. 😂❤️ And when you were angry with me, it was honestly worse than anything! 😂 I swear, I could not feel normal in school until I talked to you and somehow made things right between us. And if you ever took a day off… oh God! 😂 I cannot even explain how terribly that day would go for me. It genuinely felt like my whole world had come to an end. 😭😂❤️

Your scolding, your advice, your jokes, and the way you cared about your students—at that time, these things may have seemed normal, but now that I am far away from school, I realize how special those moments really were.

You did not just teach me; you also became a part of one of the most beautiful chapters of my life. The education, advice, and memories I received from you will always have a special place in my heart. ❤️

Happy Teachers' Day, Ma'am! 🌹
May Allah always keep you happy, healthy, and successful. Ameen. ❤️

With lots of respect and beautiful memories,

From: Um-e-Habiba 🌷
